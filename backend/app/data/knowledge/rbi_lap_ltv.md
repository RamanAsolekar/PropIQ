# RBI Guidance — Loan Against Property (LAP) LTV Norms

Source: RBI Master Direction on Loans and Advances (LAP context), 2024 reference.

## Residential property LTV bands
- Loans up to ₹30 lakh: maximum LTV 90%.
- Loans above ₹30 lakh and up to ₹75 lakh: maximum LTV 80%.
- Loans above ₹75 lakh: maximum LTV 75% (RBI cap for higher-ticket housing exposure).

## Commercial and other property
- Commercial shop / office collateral: maximum LTV 65%.
- Residential plot: maximum LTV 70%.
- Industrial (warehouse / factory): maximum LTV 55% due to lower liquidity and specialized use.

## Risk and provisioning
- Stressed collateral where LTV breaches internal limits should be tagged for
  enhanced monitoring and considered for Stage 2 / Stage 3 provisioning under
  Ind AS 109 (Expected Credit Loss).
- For retail LAP in India, lenders generally do not call for additional collateral
  mid-tenure; defensive actions are freezing top-ups, enhanced EMI monitoring, and
  provisioning.

## Title and legal
- Properties without clear title, with encumbrance/lien indicators, or pending
  zoning approvals require full legal due diligence before sanction and attract
  conservative LTV haircuts.
- RERA registration is a positive legal-clarity signal for under-construction and
  recently completed residential projects.
