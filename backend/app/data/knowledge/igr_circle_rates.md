# IGR / Circle Rate (Ready Reckoner) Notes

Source: State registration authority ready-reckoner (IGR Maharashtra, Karnataka
Kaveri) 2024-25 reference for Pune, Mumbai, Bangalore.

## What circle rates are
- The circle rate (guidance value / ready-reckoner rate) is the statutory minimum
  per-sqft value set by the state for stamp-duty and registration purposes.
- It acts as a regulatory FLOOR for valuation. Market value typically exceeds the
  circle rate by a zone-tier multiplier (prime > mid > peripheral).

## Use in collateral valuation
- A model price estimate below ~85% of the circle rate is a red flag (price below
  circle rate) and warrants review — collateral may be mispriced or distressed.
- Circle rates lag actual market conditions by 12-24 months; they should anchor
  but not solely determine valuation.

## Zone tiers
- Prime localities carry the highest circle rates and the strongest resale demand.
- Peripheral localities carry the lowest rates, longer time-to-liquidate, and
  deeper distress discounts in forced-sale scenarios.
