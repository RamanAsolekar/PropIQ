# Poonawalla Fincorp — Internal LAP Credit Policy (Collateral)

Internal policy notes for collateral acceptability in LAP underwriting.

## Conservative internal LTV (tighter than RBI maximums)
- Prime zone collateral: internal cap 70% LTV.
- Mid zone collateral: internal cap 65% LTV.
- Peripheral zone collateral: internal cap 55% LTV.

## Liquidity-linked dynamic LTV
- Highly liquid collateral (Resale Potential Index >= 75, percentile band P90+):
  permitted +10% LTV boost, capped at 85% internal maximum.
- Illiquid collateral (RPI < 50, band P10/P20): apply -15% LTV penalty.
- High NPA-risk liquidity signal: strictly cap LTV at 45%.

## Risk-flag haircuts
- Two or more high-severity flags: cap LTV at 50%.
- One high-severity flag: apply conservative LTV (cap 60%).
- Any other risk flags: apply a 5% LTV haircut.

## Collateral acceptability
- Acceptable: clear title, freehold, RERA-registered (where applicable), standard
  configuration (2BHK/3BHK), moderate-to-high resale potential.
- Marginal: leasehold, high building age (>30 years), non-standard configuration,
  or one outstanding legal item pending verification.
- Use the 90-day distress (auction) value as the security base for max-loan-on-distress.
