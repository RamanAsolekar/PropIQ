# PropIQ Valuation Model — Model Card

Governance artifact for the PropIQ collateral valuation model. Required for
NBFC model-risk-management sign-off. Update on every Production promotion.

## Model details
- **Name / version:** PropIQ AVM + Multi-Model Ensemble (`feature_schema 2.0.0`)
- **Type:** XGBoost quantile regression (P10/P50/P90) on log price-per-sqft,
  reconciled with a sales-comparison (kNN) estimate and an income-capitalization
  estimate. IsolationForest for anomaly flags. SHAP for attribution.
- **Owner:** Collateral ML team. **Registry:** `backend/data/models/registry.json`
  + MLflow (`MLFLOW_TRACKING_URI`). Champion/challenger with gated promotion.
- **Features:** 22 canonical features, single source of truth in
  `app/ml/features.py` (used identically at train and serve — no skew).

## Intended use
- **In scope:** Decision-SUPPORT for LAP collateral valuation & liquidity in
  Pune / Mumbai / Bangalore. A human underwriter remains in the loop.
- **Out of scope / do NOT use for:** Autonomous credit decisions; geographies or
  asset types outside the trained set; final sanction without legal/technical
  due diligence.

## Training data ⚠️
- **CURRENT:** 100% SYNTHETIC, generated by a rule-based pricing formula anchored
  to published circle rates (`app/ml/data_generator.py`). Reported CV MAPE
  (~7.3%) measures the model reproducing its own generator — **NOT real-world
  accuracy.** It must not be quoted to a credit committee as field accuracy.
- **TARGET:** Replace with real registry/IGR transactions + internal historical
  valuations + realized auction recoveries. The realized-outcome loop
  (`/api/v1/outcomes`, `prediction_logs` + `realized_outcomes` tables) is the
  mechanism that accumulates this ground truth.

## Evaluation
- **Offline (synthetic):** 5-fold CV MAPE, R², and **P10–P90 interval coverage**
  (currently ~63% vs 80% target — quantile band is under-calibrated; the
  ensemble layer widens the band by cross-approach disagreement to compensate).
- **True performance (the metric that matters):** `/api/v1/ml/performance`
  computes real MAPE, bias, and coverage against realized outcomes once recorded.
- **Validation gate:** `app/ml/pipelines.validation_pipeline` blocks promotion
  unless holdout MAPE ≤ 15% AND P10–P90 coverage ≥ 70%.

## Monitoring
- **Drift:** PSI per feature + Evidently report (`/api/v1/ml/drift`). PSI ≥ 0.25
  triggers gated retraining (nightly Celery beat).
- **Performance:** tracked against realized outcomes as they arrive.

## Limitations & risks
- Synthetic training → predictions are model-of-a-formula, not market truth.
- **Fair-lending:** locality is a strong driver and can proxy socio-economic
  status. Bias testing by locality/asset-class is REQUIRED before production
  lending use (not yet implemented — open governance item).
- Comps and price trends are partly synthetic; treat as indicative.

## Responsible AI
- Every prediction is SHAP-explainable; a plain-English customer letter is
  generated. Predictions are logged with their feature vector + model version
  for full auditability and reproducibility (data_hash per training run).
